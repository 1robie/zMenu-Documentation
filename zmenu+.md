# 👍 zMenu+

**zMenu+** is the official add-on for zMenu, expanding the plugin with a wide range of additional features. You can purchase zMenu+ on [Spigot](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/115533/) or through [Minecraft Inventory Builder](https://minecraft-inventory-builder.com/account-upgrade) with the Premium account upgrade.

#### Why does zMenu exist ?

zMenu is a free and open-source plugin that requires a significant amount of time to maintain and support the community. **zMenu+** helps financially sustain the development of zMenu while also giving you access to private support for the plugin.

#### **Do I need to purchase zMenu+ to use zMenu?**

No, you do not need to purchase **zMenu+** to use zMenu. **zMenu+** adds very specific features, but all essential functionalities will always be available for free in zMenu.
